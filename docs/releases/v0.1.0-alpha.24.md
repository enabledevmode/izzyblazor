The `izzy` command and the `dotnet new` starter arrive as two new packages, with a free account that adds app starters, blocks and a CRM kit. New components include Kanban, TreeList, TileLayout, Lightbox, Comment, Mentions and PageHeader. Four changes need edits in an app on alpha.23; they are listed first.

## Breaking changes

- **ListView:** an item's content goes in `ItemTemplate`, not `ChildContent`.
- **Charts:** the default palette is `Brand`, which starts at the theme's brand color. Charts of two to four series take their colors from `--ib-chart-of2-*` to `--ib-chart-of4-*`, so an app that overrides `--ib-chart-1` to `--ib-chart-8` sets those too. Falls in waterfall and price series default to `--ib-chart-6`, not `--ib-chart-8`.
- **IzzyText:** `MoveCancelled` is `MoveCanceled`.
- **`For` parameters:** a bare value is an id, and `#id` works too; a value starting with `.` or `[` is a CSS selector, which only `ContextMenu For` and `Menu ContextFor` accept. Other components say so when given one.

## Changed

- **Icons** draw at a 2-unit stroke by default, as Lucide does (1.5 before). `--ib-icon-stroke` and `StrokeWidth` still set another.
- **Dark mode:** cards and other surfaces are a step lighter than the page.
- **Charts:** Show data opens the table in a popover over the chart.
- **Dialog:** top and bottom sheets are centered and 40rem wide at most. A dialog with a footer bar gets a header bar and a close button, and its body scrolls between them.
- **DataGrid:** a row's menu button is a horizontal ellipsis, an empty grid shows only its empty state, and a new row's dialog saves with `AddText`.

## New packages

- **IzzyBlazor.Cli:** the `izzy` command (`dotnet tool install -g IzzyBlazor.Cli --prerelease`).
  - `izzy new starter blazor` makes an app, and `izzy init` sets up IzzyBlazor in an app you have: a Blazor Web App, or an MVC or Razor Pages app.
  - `izzy add crud <Model>` reads a model class from your source and writes a page whose grid adds, edits and deletes its records, on Entity Framework or an in-memory store.
  - `izzy add <item>` adds a block or page, at its own place or at a line you name (`--at <file>:<line>`). `--dry-run`, `--diff` and `--json` show the changes before anything is written.
  - `izzy login` signs the machine in to a free IzzyBlazor account. In CI, set `IZZY_KEY`.
- **IzzyBlazor.Templates:** `dotnet new starter-blazor`, Microsoft's Blazor Web App with IzzyBlazor's components and theme, in every render mode with the same options.

## Free with an account

- **Six app starters:** sidebar, collapsible rail, top navigation, website, documentation site and inbox.
- **Fifteen blocks:** hero, features, pricing, testimonials, team, FAQ, articles, contact, sign-up, footer, legal, cookies, maintenance, access denied and empty state.
- **Izzy CRM:** a whole CRM kit with every page static: contacts, companies, a deal pipeline with a board, activities and reports, with a team, roles, an audit log and sample data.

## New components

- **Kanban:** a board of cards in columns, with lanes, search and sort. People add, edit and delete cards in a dialog, and add, rename, delete and reorder columns. `OnMove` runs in every render mode.
- **TreeList:** rows that form a tree in a grid's columns, with editing, branch selection, rollups, virtual rows, exports that keep the tree, rows loaded a level at a time, and moving a row by dragging or from a dialog.
- **TileLayout** and **Tile:** a dashboard of tiles placed by column and row, which people drag and resize; it has an edit mode that works without script.
- **Lightbox:** pictures that open full size in a viewer.
- **Comment:** a comment in a discussion, with replies nested inside it.
- **Mentions** and **MentionText:** a text area where @ suggests names at the caret, and the text shown with its mentions.
- **PageHeader:** a page's title, description, media and actions, whose buttons float on a phone. **SectionHeader** heads a section of a page.
- **ActionSheet:** a few actions on one thing in a sheet from the bottom.
- **ConfirmDialog:** one dialog that asks before any of several buttons, menu items or links act.
- **ErrorGuard:** keeps the rest of a page working when one part throws.
- **FieldRepeater:** rows of the same fields that people add and remove, posting as a list.
- **ShowMore:** long content cut to its first lines, with a Show more button.
- **RelativeTime:** a time as how long ago, kept current.
- **BrowserFrame:** a screenshot inside a browser window.
- **SidebarAccount:** the signed-in person's row in a sidebar's footer, with its menu.
- **VisuallyHidden:** words for screen readers that the screen doesn't show.

## DataGrid

- **`SelectOnClick`** selects rows by clicking them.
- **`DataGridFilter`** puts a column's filter in the grid's toolbar, and the grid's and Scheduler's toolbar parts can sit outside their component with `For`.
- **`PropertyColumn`** takes a cell template and keeps the property's sort, filter and editing.
- **Phones:** columns can hide only on phones, and Columns and Export move into the toolbar's menu.
- `AddText` labels the Add button, `RowActions="false"` drops the actions column, and the edit and add dialogs take a return address when opened from another page.
- Cells stay on one line, with the whole text in a tooltip when it's cut off.

## Charts

- **Theme palettes:** Brand, Contrast, Okabe-Ito and Calm, each with sets for two to four series, and `ChartPalette.Emphasis`.
- **Buttons outside the chart:** `ChartDownload`, `ChartResetZoom`, `ChartZoom` and `ChartPause` act on a chart by `For`.
- `BarWidth` on bar series, and a reference line named in the legend (`InLegend`).

## Forms

- **`Honeypot`** and `OnSpam` on `Form`.
- **`DisabledUntil`** on `Button`: a button that waits for a checkbox or a text field.
- **`SubmitOnChange`** on `Checkbox` and `Switch` posts their form as they change, and `HideLabel` leaves only the box.
- **`HintStatus`** on fields: a hint that states a status. `RadioGroup` takes `ItemDescription`.
- **Combobox:** an action at the end of the list, with `ListHeader` and `ListFooter`.
- **FileUpload:** a form posts its files without `Multipart`; `Size="Sm"` and a `Preview` beside the zone.
- Fixed: on a static post an optional text field left empty is no value, so `[Phone]` and `[Url]` accept a blank; a static form that saves no longer shows its cleared fields as invalid; a field that throws on a static page is caught inside the form.

## Other changes

- **Toolbar:** `FloatBelow` makes an item a floating button on a phone, items that float together share a speed dial, and `Overflow` can change by breakpoint.
- **Scheduler:** the month and the agenda are laid out for a phone, and `ItemHref` links an appointment to a page of its own.
- **Timeline:** done and to come, avatars, badges, a compact size, `Actions` on an item, and `TimePlacement` by breakpoint.
- **ListView:** `ItemMedia` puts an avatar or picture where the row's icon goes; `ItemAlign`.
- **Carousel:** `Dots="Thumbnails"`. **Splitter:** `OnePaneBelow`. **Card:** `Orientation` and `CardMedia Width`.
- **Layout:** `LayoutOrder` per breakpoint, `Sticky` on box-like components, `Container` `Align`, `Gutters` and size `Xs`, and `DescriptionList` columns per breakpoint.
- **AppSidebar** and **AppBrand:** a `Description` under the name, and in the rail the brand's mark is the expand button.
- **Print** on `Button`, `IconButton` and `MenuItem`; `AppShell` prints its page alone.
- **Smaller additions:** `Tag Pill`, `Image DarkSrc`, `BreadcrumbItem TextOnly`, `TabLink` `BadgeStatus` and `DisabledReason`, `EmptyState` `Status` and `Accent`, `Blockquote` attribution, `IconButton` content such as an avatar, `NoMatchesContent` on data views, and `TextBlock KeepLines`.
- Fixed: in Safari a dialog showed one line of its body; `Carousel Gap="0"` showed every slide at once; a popover closes when a link or a static form in it is used.
