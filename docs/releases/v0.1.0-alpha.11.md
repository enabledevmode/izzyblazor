Every component with regions is now put together from parts, as `Card` was in alpha.10: write the parts among the content (`DialogFooter`, `SidebarHeader`, `BannerActions`…) and leave out the `<ChildContent>` wrapper. Content outside the parts is the body; an optional body part (`DialogBody`, `SidebarBody`) gives it layout props. Buttons follow one rule: `Href` makes it a link, so `LinkButton` is gone. `HeaderNav` is now `SiteNav`, and its phone menu opens under the header. Checkboxes, switches and fieldsets take an icon.

## Changed (check when you update)

- **`LinkButton` is replaced by `Button` with `Href`.** A `Button` with `Href` is a link (`a`) and keeps everything `LinkButton` had: `External`, `HideExternalIcon`, `NewTabLabel`, and no `href` while `Disabled` or `Loading`. A link throws if it's also given `Submit`, `Reset`, `Name`, `Value`, `Form` or `FormAction`; `DialogTrigger` and `DialogClose` can't take `Href`. `IconButton` with `Href` likewise throws with `Submit` or `Reset`, and now runs `OnClick`.
- **`Dialog`:** `Header` is replaced by `DialogHeader`, and `Footer` and `DialogActions` by `DialogFooter`. `DialogFooter` is drawn where it's written, so write it last: last in the dialog, or last in a form that's last in the dialog, where its submit button submits the form with no `Form="id"`. It reaches the dialog's edges (following `ContentPadding`) and stays in view while the body scrolls. To keep it at the bottom of a side drawer when the content is short, put the content in a `DialogBody`.
- **`AppSidebar`:** `Header` and `Footer` are replaced by `SidebarHeader` and `SidebarFooter`. In the drawer, the `SidebarHeader` row is now the top bar, with the close button in it, instead of a close bar followed by the header as a second row.
- **`Alert`'s `Actions`** is replaced by `AlertActions`, written last in the alert; **`Banner`'s** by `BannerActions`; **`EmptyState`'s** by `EmptyStateActions`. **`AccordionItem`'s `Header`** is replaced by `AccordionHeader` (an item no longer throws without a `Title`: give it a `Title` or an `AccordionHeader`, since that's its name).
- **`HeaderNav` is now `SiteNav`.** Below its breakpoint the menu button opens the links in a panel under the header, which stays in view with the site's name, and the same button closes it. The sheet's own top bar, its close button and `PanelHeader` are gone (with `CloseLabel`); `PanelFooter` is `SiteNavFooter`, written among the links. The button's name is now "Menu" (`IzzyText.Menu`). CSS classes and the element are `ib-site-nav…` (were `ib-header-nav…`). In a shell with a sidebar, a `SiteNavFooter` doesn't move into the sidebar: use `SidebarFooter`.
- **The old names fail loudly for one release.** `<LinkButton>`, `<DialogActions>`, `<HeaderNav>` and the old slots written as attributes (`Footer="@..."`) are build errors (CS0619) naming the replacement. The old slots written as child elements (`<Footer>` in a `Dialog`, `<Header>` in an `AppSidebar` or `AccordionItem`, `<Actions>` in an `Alert`, `Banner` or `EmptyState`) throw when the component renders, naming the part to use, or fail to build with RZ9996 when written beside loose content. Search for `<Header>`, `<Footer>`, `<Actions>`, `PanelHeader` and `PanelFooter` rather than waiting for a page to throw.
- `CardPart`, the public base of Card's parts, is replaced by `ComponentPart`, shared by every part a component draws in its own place.
- In the collapsed sidebar rail, an `AppBrand`'s name now hides by itself (it still shows in the drawer and the full sidebar), so a brand there needs no `HideLabel`.

## New

- **`DialogBody`**: the dialog's body with `Stack`'s layout props. It fills the dialog, so a `DialogFooter` after it sits at the bottom of a side drawer; `Flush` runs it to the sides. **`SidebarBody`**: the sidebar's scrolling content with layout props.
- **`Icon` on `Checkbox`, `Switch` and `Fieldset`**: a row icon before the label (with `ControlPosition.End`, a column at the start centered on the label and hint), or a section icon at the start of a fieldset's heading. Decorative. Put icons on rows or on sections, not both.
- **`DialogFooter`** and **`AlertActions`** take `Row`'s layout props (`Justify` moves the buttons; a dialog's are at the end by default).
- **`Select`** shows the chosen text as a tooltip, so a value cut short can be read on hover.

## Fixed

- A collapsed full-height sidebar's header content is centered in its row beside an `AppHeader` (it sat at the top).
- Opening the sidebar drawer focuses its close button.
- A card part swapped in with `@if`/`else` kept showing its first content when it changed later.
