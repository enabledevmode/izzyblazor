The rest of the named slots become parts, as `Card`'s did in alpha.10: the sidebar's header and footer, the dialog's header and footer, and an alert's actions are components written among the content, so the content needs no `<ChildContent>` wrapper. Buttons follow one rule: `Href` makes it a link, so `LinkButton` is gone. Checkboxes, switches and fieldsets take an icon.

## Changed (check when you update)

- **`LinkButton` is replaced by `Button` with `Href`.** A `Button` with `Href` is a link (`a`) and keeps everything `LinkButton` had: `External`, `HideExternalIcon`, `NewTabLabel`, and no `href` while `Disabled` or `Loading`. A link throws if it's also given `Submit`, `Reset`, `Name`, `Value`, `Form` or `FormAction`; `DialogTrigger` and `DialogClose` can't take `Href`.
- **`AppSidebar`'s `Header` and `Footer` are replaced by `SidebarHeader` and `SidebarFooter`**, written directly in the sidebar, and the `<ChildContent>` wrapper around the navigation goes. In the drawer, the `SidebarHeader` row is now the top bar, with the close button in it, instead of a close bar followed by the header as a second row.
- **`Dialog`'s `Header` and `Footer` are replaced by `DialogHeader` and `DialogFooter`, and `DialogActions` by `DialogFooter`.** `DialogFooter` is drawn where it's written, so write it last: last in the dialog, or last in a form that's last in the dialog, where its submit button submits the form with no `Form="id"`. It reaches the dialog's edges (following `ContentPadding`) and stays in view while the body scrolls. In a side drawer it follows the content rather than sitting at the drawer's bottom. `DialogHeader` is drawn in the header row, so a form or cascading value it's written inside doesn't reach it.
- **`Alert`'s `Actions` is replaced by `AlertActions`**, written last in the alert.
- **The old names fail loudly for one release.** `<LinkButton>`, `<DialogActions>` and the old slots written as attributes (`Footer="@..."`) are build errors (CS0619) naming the replacement. The old slots written as child elements (`<Footer>...</Footer>` in a `Dialog`, `<Header>` in an `AppSidebar`, `<Actions>` in an `Alert`) build, because Razor passes them by name, but throw when the component renders, naming the part to use. Search for `<Header>`, `<Footer>` and `<Actions>` inside `AppSidebar`, `Dialog`, `Drawer` and `Alert` rather than waiting for a page to throw. (`Banner` and `EmptyState` keep `Actions`; `AccordionItem` keeps `Header`.)
- In the collapsed sidebar rail, an `AppBrand`'s name now hides by itself (it still shows in the drawer and the full sidebar), so a brand there needs no `HideLabel`.
- `IconButton` with `Href` throws if it's also given `Submit` or `Reset`, and runs `OnClick` on interactive pages, like `Button` with `Href`.

## New

- **`Icon` on `Checkbox`, `Switch` and `Fieldset`**: a row icon before the label (with `ControlPosition.End`, a column at the start centered on the label and hint), or a section icon at the start of a fieldset's heading. Decorative. Put icons on rows or on sections, not both.
- **`DialogFooter`** and **`AlertActions`** take `Row`'s layout props (`Justify` moves the buttons; a dialog's are at the end by default).
- **`Select`** shows the chosen text as a tooltip, so a value cut short can be read on hover.

## Fixed

- A collapsed full-height sidebar's header content is centered in its row beside an `AppHeader` (it sat at the top).
- Opening the sidebar drawer focuses its close button.
- A card part swapped in with `@if`/`else` kept showing its first content when it changed later.
