A new `Toolbar`: one row of controls that stays one row, with what doesn't fit moving into a "…" menu that works with JavaScript off. Also: the names replaced in alpha.11 are gone, checkboxes, switches, radios, segments and sliders lose `ReadOnly`, dark mode is darker, segmented controls get a raised look, and a long list of accessibility and static-page fixes. Nothing moves on the page any more when IzzyBlazor's script arrives.

## Toolbar

**`Toolbar`** holds its own items, written straight in it: `ToolbarButton`, `ToolbarIconButton`, `ToolbarButtonGroup` (of `ToolbarButton`s and `ToolbarIconButton`s; moves as one) and `ToolbarMenu` (a submenu in the "…" menu), which take the same parameters as `Button`, `IconButton`, `ButtonGroup` and `Menu`. `ToolbarText` is text that stays in the bar; `ToolbarItem` holds anything else (a field, a `SegmentedControl`, a `Tooltip`), with a `Width` and its `MenuContent`; `ToolbarSeparator` draws a line that goes with its neighbors.

```razor
<Toolbar Label="Editor">
    <ToolbarButtonGroup Label="History">
        <ToolbarIconButton Icon="Lucide.Undo2" Label="Undo" />
        <ToolbarIconButton Icon="Lucide.Redo2" Label="Redo" />
    </ToolbarButtonGroup>
    <ToolbarSeparator />
    <ToolbarButton StartIcon="Lucide.Link">Link</ToolbarButton>
    <ToolbarButton Position="ToolbarPosition.End" Overflow="ToolbarOverflow.Never" Accent="Accent.Primary" Variant="ButtonVariant.Solid">Publish</ToolbarButton>
    <ToolbarButton StartIcon="Lucide.Printer" Overflow="ToolbarOverflow.Always">Print</ToolbarButton>
</Toolbar>
```

- Every item takes **`Position`** (`ToolbarPosition.Start`, the default, `Center` or `End`), **`Priority`** (lowest moves first) and **`Overflow`** (`ToolbarOverflow.Auto`, the default; `Never` for the main action; `Always` for what only the menu needs). A button in a `ToolbarButtonGroup` takes its `Position` from the group. `ToolbarSeparator` takes `Position`.
- In the "…" menu an item keeps its `Href`, `Submit`/`Name`/`Value`/`FormAction`/`Form` and `OnClick`, so the menu works with JavaScript off: the server works out the widths at which each item moves, from its label in Inter, and writes them as container queries (with `IzzyTheme`'s nonce, or `Nonce`). The script measures the real widths and corrects them without moving anything, and makes the bar a `toolbar` with one tab stop and arrow keys. A `ToolbarIconButton`'s menu item shows its icon and its `Label`.
- Buttons default to `Ghost`, `Neutral` and `Sm`. `Wrap` wraps onto more rows instead of using a menu. `Label` (optional) names the toolbar; name each one when a page has several. `MoreLabel` names "…" (`IzzyText.MoreActions`, "More actions").
- A `ToolbarItem` moves into the menu only when it has `MenuContent`, which stands in for it there; the buttons inside it are plain `Button`s that stay with it.
- A plain `Button`, `IconButton`, `ButtonGroup` or `Menu` directly in a `Toolbar` or a `ToolbarButtonGroup` throws, naming the toolbar item to use (or `ToolbarItem`).
- The toolbar draws its items itself, so a cascading value written around an item inside the toolbar doesn't reach it, and an item an `@if` adds after the first render comes after the rest of its position.

## Changed (check when you update)

- **The names replaced in 0.1.0-alpha.11 are removed.** They were kept through alpha.12 so old markup failed to build. Gone: `LinkButton` (use `Button` with `Href`), `HeaderNav` (`SiteNav`), `DialogActions` (`DialogFooter`), `ButtonType` and the `Type` parameter of `Button`, `IconButton` and `MenuItem` (`Submit`, `Reset`), and the slot parameters `AccordionItem.Header`, `Alert`/`Banner`/`EmptyState.Actions`, `Dialog.Header`/`Footer` and `AppSidebar.Header`/`Footer`. Old slot markup now gets Razor's RZ10012 warning (an unknown element) instead of an error, so check your build warnings.
- **`Checkbox`, `Switch`, `CheckboxGroup`, `RadioGroup`, `SegmentedControl` and `Slider` have no `ReadOnly`.** HTML has no read-only checkbox, radio or range. `ReadOnly` moved off `FieldBase` onto the fields that keep it (`TextField`, `TextArea`, `NumberField`, `ColorPicker`, `Select`, `Combobox`, `MultiSelect`). On the six it's a build error, and a `readonly` attribute passed any other way throws. In a read-only `Form` they're disabled and hidden inputs post their values. Use `Disabled`.
- **`Heading` throws on `As`.** `Level` sets its element, and `As` was silently ignored. Use `TextBlock` for text that looks like a heading but isn't one.
- **`Switch` refuses `Indeterminate`** (it throws).
- **`FilterSelect` and `FilterCheckbox` need a `Label`** (they were named by the query key). Their first option defaults to "{Label}: All".
- **More mistakes throw:** a `Card` with `Href` and no `Title`; an `AppMain` whose `id` differs from its `AppShell`'s `MainId`.
- **Dark mode is darker.** Every gray set's dark surface steps down: neutral surface `#0d0d0f` (was `#141416`), stone `#0e0f10`, slate `#0f1218`, with the page, containers, raised fill, border and outline moving with it. Text and outlines keep their contrast. A hex `Gray`'s dark ladder follows, and `BrandPalette` checks against the new surfaces.
- **Segmented controls have a filled track and a raised chosen item.** `SegmentedControl`, `ColorSchemeSwitch Segmented` and segmented `Tabs`, from `--ib-color-segment` and the new `--ib-color-on-segment`, `--ib-color-segment-edge` and `--ib-color-segment-track`. Segmented `Tabs` drop the track's outline.
- **Disabled fields drop their border**, as do disabled add-ons, `ColorPicker`'s and `MultiSelect`'s boxes and the segmented track (read-only keeps it); also in a disabled fieldset. A disabled control's field icons dim (a `Select`'s chevron).
- **`Switch` follows `Size`:** tracks are 28×16, 36×20 or 44×24px (were 36×20 at every size).
- **`NavLabel` heads its group:** 24px above and 8px below (was 12px above), in the text color instead of the faintest one.
- **A `Code` block's fill is quieter in light mode** (surface-container-low); inline code is unchanged.
- **`TextArea AutoGrow` starts at `Rows` lines** instead of a fixed two field heights.
- **A `Tabs` tab is a `span`** holding its radio and a label stretched over it (so `role="tab"` isn't on a `label`). Check CSS of your own that targets the tab's label.
- **`MultiSelect` draws its chips on the server** and reads `RendererInfo`, so a bUnit test rendering one must set it.
- **A static `TextField` no longer writes a posted password back** into its HTML; the field is empty after a failed post.
- **`AppShell` reserves the scrollbar strip only with JavaScript off.** With script the page has a scrollbar only while it scrolls, and is padded while a dialog, drawer or panel locks it, so nothing shifts.
- **`NavItem` and `TabLink` with a query** are current only when the page's query is the same (in any order), and never for pages below. In a `Tabs` nav, when a link with a query matches, links without one give way.
- **Focus moves on when the focused control is removed** (a `Tag`'s remove button, a row's Delete): to the next control in the same container, else the one before, else the next on the page.
- `Popover`'s centered and side placements size to their content and fall back below or above the trigger when neither side has room; `Tooltip`'s flip at the window's edges.
- `izzyblazor.css` no longer carries an inline source map, Debug included (1.1 MB down to 322 KB).

## New

- **`Toolbar`**, `ToolbarButton`, `ToolbarIconButton`, `ToolbarButtonGroup`, `ToolbarMenu`, `ToolbarText`, `ToolbarItem`, `ToolbarSeparator`, `ToolbarPosition` and `ToolbarOverflow` (above).
- **`CheckboxGroup SelectAll`**: a box that checks or clears every option, showing a dash when some are checked; `SelectAllText` ("Select all"). Needs script (static pages too); with JavaScript off it isn't shown. **`CheckboxGroup ItemDescription`**: an option's description, apart from its name.
- **`IconButton`**: `Name`, `Value`, `Form` and `FormAction`, as on `Button` (they throw with `Href`).
- **`ColorPicker PresetText`** names the presets. **`FieldError Id`**. **`FieldFrame AnnounceRequired`**.
- **`ModeSupportAttribute`** on every component, saying what works with JavaScript off, on static pages with the script and on interactive pages. It changes nothing at run time; the docs site shows it. Every `…Changed` callback outside the form fields (`@bind-Open`, `Table`'s `@bind-Sort` and the rest) is marked interactive only.
- `IzzyText`: `MoreActions`, `SelectAll`, `Chosen`, `Required`, `Confirm`, `Search`, `SkipToContent`, `CodeBlock`, `ValueInvalid` and `NumberInvalid`, for text that was hard-coded English.

## Fixed

- **Nothing moves when IzzyBlazor's script arrives.** Parts shown or hidden by the script moved the page 7-82px about 200ms after it loaded; CSS now decides by `@media (scripting)`, `MultiSelect` draws its chips on the server, and `Combobox` keeps its select until ready. An on-demand element whose code can't load is laid out as with no script.
- Accessibility: a code block is a group named by its `Title` (or "Code"); a loading `IconButton` reads "Loading Delete"; a `DialogHeader` is a level-2 heading, and `DialogService` dialogs without a title are named by `DialogOptions.Label` or the message; a labeled `Divider` is read as its text; `Fieldset`'s `Description` describes the group; `Combobox` and `MultiSelect` announce "No matches" through a status beside the list; copy buttons announce "Copied" through a live region; `MultiSelect`'s input is described by its chosen options; `FormErrors` is read once (no longer also `role="alert"`); `NavLabel` and `NavDivider` are valid list items; a required `CheckboxGroup` says "(required)"; `CheckboxGroup`, `RadioGroup` and `SegmentedControl` take focus after a failed static post; `TextArea`'s count is linked as its description; a required `Combobox` or `MultiSelect` sets `aria-required`.
- The skip link moves focus to the main area under enhanced navigation too, and is inert while the sidebar drawer is open. `SidebarToggle` and the close links are plain links with JavaScript off and buttons with `aria-expanded` with script.
- Static pages: a closed `?dialog=` stays out of the address after an enhanced load; a dismissed `Alert` stays closed after a post; the first edit of a field with errors from the last post hides them; Escape closes a server-opened `Dialog` or `Drawer` where `closedby` is supported (Chrome and Edge 134, Firefox 141).
- `Combobox`: changing the choice with `Search` on an interactive page keeps it (the select fell back to the placeholder and posted nothing); Enter and leaving wait for search results; Enter with the list open never submits the form; duplicate values show once; server search reopens on the first results, not the last search's.
- `FilterForm`: inputs of your own (a date) apply when they change; an interactive page keeps its page number on load; Back and Forward forget what was being typed; Clear focuses the first filter; the button area keeps its height while empty.
- `Checkbox` and `Switch`: links in a label can be clicked.
- `Slider`: quick key presses on an interactive page aren't undone.
- `ToastRegion`: a toast shown before `NavigateTo` on an interactive page stays, and a toast reusing an earlier one's element gets its own timer.
- Dialogs, drawers and panels reach the window's edge on `AppShell` pages, with the backdrop covering the scrollbar strip.
- `AppBrand` with an icon in a `SidebarHeader` re-rendered forever (a full-height sidebar hung the server).
- `Tabs`: after enhanced navigation the selection follows the server's markup again; arrows from a clicked disabled tab go to the next enabled one.
- `IconButton` in a `Tooltip` leaves out its native `title`, so hovering shows one tooltip. A hidden `Tooltip` without script no longer makes the page scroll sideways.
- `Card`: `CardFooter` stays at the bottom of a stretched card; a linked card's media sit above its link.
- `MultiSelect`: chips follow the field's `Size` and density; a disabled one looks disabled with script and its chips lose their remove buttons; the chevron stays beside the first row of chips; chips that fill a row don't add an empty one.
- `SegmentedControl` with `HideLabel` has no space above it, so it lines up in a row. A read-only `ColorPicker` shows its hex box so it can be focused.
- A plain `[EmailAddress]` or `[Url]` no longer sends .NET's English message, so the browser's own shows.
