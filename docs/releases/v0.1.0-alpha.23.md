Nine new chart types (bullet, linear gauge, range bar and range area, histogram, candlestick, OHLC, box plot and violin), a crosshair, PNG downloads of any chart, and export formats of your own on DataGrid. IzzyBlazor now builds on .NET 11 as well as .NET 10. Nothing was removed or renamed since alpha.22.

## Changed

- **Splitter:** a click on a bar opens its menu (Wider, Narrower, Reset size, Collapse), and so do right-click, a long press, Shift+F10 and the menu key. A double-click no longer collapses a pane; use the menu or the collapse button.

## New components

- **BulletChart:** each row's value against its target over ranges in grays, such as a KPI scorecard; a scale per row (`Max`) or one shared, bands per row (`Ranges`) or for every row (`GaugeBand`), and `Orientation.Vertical`.
- **LinearGauge:** a value on a straight scale, as a bar, a marker or a needle into thick bands, with ticks, a target, `Reversed` and a vertical scale.
- **GaugePointer:** another value on the same `Gauge` or `LinearGauge`, such as gusts beside the mean wind.
- **Chart series:** `ChartRangeBarSeries` and `ChartRangeAreaSeries` (each item's low to its high), `ChartHistogramSeries` (values counted in round bins, by a group with `SeriesBy`), `ChartCandlestickSeries` and `ChartOhlcSeries` (prices; OHLC without `Open` is high-low-close), `ChartBoxPlotSeries` (quartiles, Tukey or min-max whiskers, outliers and the mean from raw values), `ChartBoxSummarySeries` (boxes from numbers already worked out) and `ChartViolinSeries` (each group's density, with a box inside).

## Charts

- **Crosshair** on `Chart`: lines at the item and its nearest value, with badges on both axes.
- **Download** saves a chart as a PNG from a button across from its title, on every chart type but Gauge and Sparkline. A button of your own takes `data-ib-chart-download="<chart id>"`, and a file input with `data-ib-chart-png="<chart id>"` sends the chart's PNG with a form, such as for a PDF the server builds.
- **`SkipGaps`** on `ChartArgumentAxis` puts dates one step apart, so weekends and holidays take no room. Dates spaced by item are labeled where days, months and years begin.
- **`Target`** on `ChartBarSeries`: a tick across each bar at its target.
- **`Sweep`** on `PieChart`: slices round part of the circle; a half circle takes only its height.
- **Zoom in and Zoom out** buttons beside Reset zoom, and a chart that only follows a synced group's view shows Reset zoom alone.
- **Gauges:** `Palette` colors the bands in order, grays by default, and a band's `Accent` is optional. `ChartPalette.Tints` steps from a light tint to the full color, for bands.
- Fixed: a click in a zoomable chart no longer collapses its view; zoomed-in date labels at either end stay inside the chart; drill-down keeps the page where it is.

## DataGrid

- **Export formats of your own:** `DataGridExportFormat` among the columns adds a format to the export menu, its link carrying the grid's filter, sort, search and columns. `ExportHref` gives that address for a button of your own, `Exportable="false"` keeps a column out of the file, and `DataExport.Read` hands a format the columns and rows that CSV and Excel write.
- **`DataFields.ByName`** builds fields keyed and read as `DynamicColumn` does.
- A group chip's menu moves its level left and right, as Alt+Left and Alt+Right do, and a grouped column's menu moves its group.

## Other changes

- **.NET 11:** the library builds and runs on .NET 11 as well as .NET 10.
- **`NavLabel`** takes `Href` and `Current`, so a `NavMenu` heading links to its group's page.
- **ReconnectDialog** titles a paused session as paused.
