Reserving the founding price now includes IzzyBlazor Pro as a preview, `izzy audit` gives each finding a verdict, and `izzy feedback` tells us what works and what doesn't.

## Pro preview

- **Reserving the founding price includes Pro as a preview,** free until Pro goes on sale. Sign in to your account with the address you reserved, then run `izzy login`. A machine that is already signed in gets it the next time it runs a Pro command.
- `izzy account` says "Plan: Pro preview", and `izzy account --json` gives the plan as data: `free`, `preview` or `pro`.
- The preview ends when Pro goes on sale, unless you buy Pro then, or when you cancel the reservation. The code you generated stays yours.
- A sign-in past its date keeps the account's templates and not Pro: Pro works offline for the 30 days a sign-in lasts.

## CLI

- **`izzy audit` gives each finding a verdict (Pro):** real, needs a person, accepted, demo page or false positive, each with its reason and a confidence. The report groups the findings by verdict, and `results.json` has them as data.
- **Your own decisions go in `.izzy/audit.json`:** `accepted` lists the findings you accept (rule, selector, pages, reason), and `demoPages` the pages that show components for their own sake.
- **`--format tasks`** writes `tasks.md`, a checklist of what to fix and what to look at.
- **`--baseline <results.json>`** compares with a past run: new findings are marked, and fixed ones are checked off. `--fail-on real` ends with exit code 5 on a real finding, and `--fail-on new` on a real finding the baseline lacks.
- **`izzy feedback` (Pro and its preview)** asks what happened and which command it was about, shows the post, and sends it on a yes to the [Pro preview feedback](https://github.com/enabledevmode/izzyblazor/discussions/categories/pro-preview-feedback) discussions, without your name, email or account. It refuses text that holds a password, a key or a real email address. `izzy feedback "<what happened>" --about "<command>" --yes` sends it in one line.
- **With `--fail-on`, a page that didn't open or answered with an error ends `izzy audit` with exit code 1.** It passed before.
- A page that leads somewhere else, such as a sign-in page, is reported with the address that was checked.
- Stopping izzy during an audit (Ctrl+C, a build server's cancel) ends the browser and the app it started, and removes the browser's profile.
- A browser that can't be started, closes or stops answering is a sentence and exit code 1; it was a stack trace.
- Two of one component on a page are two findings; they were counted as one.
- Text from the audited page can no longer write Markdown of its own into `report.md`.
- An emoji at the place where an element's HTML is cut no longer fails the report.
- The audit's dates and its folder's name no longer follow the machine's language.
