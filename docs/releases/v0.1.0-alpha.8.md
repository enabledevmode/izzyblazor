Fixes from an independent review of alpha.7: a switch or checkbox at the end of its row now works in horizontal forms and sits on whole pixels, a templated `Select` no longer squashes images and badges, and `Fieldset Dividers` handles nested fieldsets. No renames.

## Changed (check when you update)

- **`Fieldset Dividers` with a `Gap` set only at breakpoints** (`Gap="new() { Md = 6 }"`): below the first breakpoint the rows had no gap but 16px above each line; now the line has no space on either side there, matching the gap. Give `Gap` a base value to space them.

## Fixed

- A switch or checkbox at the end of its row (`ControlPosition="ControlPosition.End"`) in a horizontal `Form` (640px and up): the hint and errors moved into the switch's column and pushed the switch away from the end, and in a form with `Columns` the switch sat above its label. The row now takes the form's whole width, as it does elsewhere.
- At the end of its row, the switch or box sat half a pixel off in most rows (the label and hint had odd line heights). Their line heights now round to even pixels, so the control lands on whole pixels at every size.
- A templated `Select` (`ItemTemplate`) squashed images, badges and avatars in the closed field when the value was too long. They keep their size now, and only text in its own element (such as a `<span>`) shrinks and ends in "…". Text not in its own element is cut off at the field's edge, without "…".
- `Fieldset Dividers`: a nested `Fieldset` among the rows had its heading drawn on the line. The heading now sits below the line.
- A disabled `Switch`'s label is grayed, like a disabled checkbox's.
- `Checkbox` and `Switch` put the end-position class after `Class`; your `Class` now comes last, as on every other field.

## Docs

- `Fieldset Dividers` puts the `Gap` above and below each line (alpha.7's notes said "midway in the gap"). Each direct child is a row: don't put `Divider`s between them, a row's own `Padding` replaces the space above its line, and a first row hidden with `HiddenFrom` or `VisibleFrom` still counts as first.
- `ControlPosition` is on `Checkbox` and `Switch` only, not on the options of `RadioGroup` or `CheckboxGroup`.
