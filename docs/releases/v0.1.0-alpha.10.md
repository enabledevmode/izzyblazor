Cards are put together from parts instead of named slots: `CardBody`, `CardFooter`, `CardHeader`, `CardActions` and `CardMedia`. Content between a card's tags is its body, with no `<ChildContent>` wrapper, and the body and footer take layout props. A footer can be a colored bar across the card's bottom edge, and `Dividers` splits a card into sections.

## Changed (check when you update)

- **`Card`'s `Actions`, `Footer`, `Media` and `Header` slots are replaced by `CardActions`, `CardFooter`, `CardMedia` and `CardHeader`**, written directly in the card, and the `<ChildContent>` wrapper around the body goes: content between the card's tags is the body.
- **Search for the old slots: not all of them fail to build.** A card that still wraps its body in `<ChildContent>` fails with error RZ9996. Without it, an old `<Actions>`, `<Footer>`, `<Media>` or `<Header>` builds with only warning RZ10012 and renders as a plain HTML element (`<footer>` and `<header>` become page landmarks), and `Footer="@..."` or `Header="@..."` written as an attribute builds silently.
- `CardMedia`, `CardHeader` and `CardActions` are drawn at the card's level, wherever they're written: a form or cascading value they're written inside doesn't reach them. Put a form's buttons in a `CardFooter`, or the form around the whole card.
- A `Filled` or `Elevated` card's background is now the `--ib-card-bg` variable (set per variant), so a custom background can be set with it.

## New

- **`CardBody`**: the body with `Stack`'s layout props (`Gap`, `Align`, `Justify`, `Direction`). Optional: content written straight in the card is its body too. `Flush` runs its content to the card's edges, for a table or a divided list; a `Table` in it takes the card's background, loses its own frame and lines its columns and pager up with the card's padding.
- **`CardFooter`**: the bottom row with `Row`'s props (`Justify="Justify.End"` puts buttons at the end), drawn last. It spans the card's width and bottom edge, so `Bg="Color.SurfaceContainerLow" BorderTop="1"` makes it a bar across the bottom, padded evenly.
- **`CardHeader`**: custom heading content above the title, such as an avatar row, beside `CardActions`.
- **`Card Dividers`**: each `CardBody` is a section, with a line across the card between sections, as in a settings page.

## Fixed

- A card's actions now line up with the edge in right-to-left pages too (their offset used physical left/right margins).
