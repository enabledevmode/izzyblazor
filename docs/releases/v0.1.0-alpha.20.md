The biggest release yet: a DataGrid, a ListView with drag and drop, date and time fields, a Calendar, SplitButton, Diff, FilterBuilder and ReconnectDialog, and a data layer (`DataRequest`, a filter tree, selection) that Table, ListView and DataGrid share. Table's data API is renamed for it, so check the Breaking list before upgrading.

## Breaking

- **Delete `app.MapIzzyBlazor();` from Program.cs.** The color scheme switch no longer needs an endpoint, and the method is gone. `ColorSchemeSwitch` is hidden without script; the page follows the device or the saved cookie.
- **`TableRequest<T>` is `DataRequest<T>`.** `SortKey` and `Descending` are a `Sort` list of `SortField`; the `Filters` dictionary is a `Filter` tree (read a column's value with `request.Filter?.Find("key")?.Value`); `Page` and `PageSize` are `Skip` and `Take` (`Page` is still there).
- **`TableResult<T>` is `DataResult<T>`**, and `TotalItems` is `Total`, an `int?` (null when the source doesn't count, for cursor paging).
- **`Table.Filters` and `FiltersChanged` (`@bind-Filters`) are `Filter` and `FilterChanged` (`@bind-Filter`)**, a `Filter` tree rather than a dictionary.
- **`Table.Selectable` is `SelectionMode`, and `@bind-SelectedKeys` is `@bind-Selection`**, a `Selection` (keys, or all matching the filter except some keys). A static form posts `{name}.Keys`.
- **`Table.FilterPlacement` and the `FilterPlacement` enum are removed.** Table filters open from the header; the filter row is the DataGrid's (`FilterRow`).
- **`PropertyColumn.FilterMultiple` and `TemplateColumn.FilterMultiple` are removed.** An enum or bool column's filter is always a list of checkboxes; `FilterList` gives any column one.
- **Number filters are ranges**, so old links like `?f-qty=3` no longer filter.
- **`IzzyText.All` is removed** (the filter select it labeled is gone).
- **CSS classes `ib-table-footer`, `ib-table-footer-info`, `ib-table-summary` and `ib-table-page-size*` are `ib-data-*`**, shared by Table, ListView and DataGrid. The old header filter's classes (`ib-table-filter-trigger`, `-summary`, `-chevron`, `-multiple`, `-actions`) are gone.
- **`ModeSupportAttribute`** (for documenting components) takes `Static`, `Interactive` and `NoScript`, with `Notes` and `NoScriptNotes`, in place of `Off`, `Static` and `Live`. The docs speak of two render modes, Static and Interactive, with no script as a fallback.

## New components

- **DataGrid**, on Table's base: keyboard navigation, frozen, resizable and reorderable columns, a column menu and picker, a toolbar (`DataGridSearch`, `DataGridExport` to CSV and Excel, `DataGridColumnPicker`, `DataGridAdd`, `DataGridFilterBuilder`), a filter row, virtual rows, row and context menus (`ActionColumn`, `RowAction`, `EditAction`, `DeleteAction`), editing in a dialog, in the row or by cell (`EditLayout`, `EditField`, `EditFormTemplate`), aggregates, grouping with a group bar, row details, columns that hide by `Priority` on narrow screens, and a layout kept in a cookie. Its docs have topic pages of their own.
- **ListView**: a list or cards (`Columns`) of items with the Table's paging, loading, errors and selection; item actions and a ⋯ menu; reordering by a drag handle, the whole item or a long press, with Move menu items and Alt+arrow keys; **DropZoneGroup** moves items between lists (a kanban board).
- **DateField** (with `Type` of `Date`, `Month` or `Year`, and a calendar to choose from), **DateRangeField** (months side by side, presets), **MultiDateField**, **TimeField** (a list of times, or boxes), **DurationField** and **Calendar** (`DateDisabled`, `DayClass`).
- **SplitButton** and **ToolbarSplitButton**: a main action with a menu beside it.
- **FilterBuilder**: nested groups of rules built from fields; grids read it from `?filter=`.
- **FilterDate**, a date beside FilterSearch, FilterSelect and FilterCheckbox.
- **Diff**: two texts compared line by line, unified or side by side.
- **ReconnectDialog**: Blazor's reconnect box in the library's dialog, themed in dark mode. Put it in App.razor after `Routes`.
- **AsideToggle**, and a page's own side panel: `AppAside` takes `SmallScreen`, `Label` and `CloseLabel`, and below its breakpoint is hidden, goes after the main area, or opens as a drawer.

## Data

- `DataRequest`, `DataResult`, the `Filter` tree (`FieldFilter`, `FilterOperator`, `FilterCase`), `DataField` and `DataFields.Of`; requests serialize, and `ToODataQuery` writes an OData query. `request.Apply(query)` works on lists and through EF Core. `DataGroupRequest` and `DataAggregate` do the same for groups and aggregates.
- Column filters are typed, choose their operators (`FilterOperators`), take a list of looked-up values (`FilterValues`) or controls of your own (`FilterTemplate`). A day on a field with a time means the whole day.
- `Selection`, `SelectionMode`, `SelectAllMode`, `ItemSelectable` and `SelectionActions`: a selection bar with "Select all N", kept across pages and enhanced navigation, Shift ranges, also on static pages.

## Table

- `Paging`: cursor paging, Load more, and loading on scroll (`DataPaging`); `ReloadAsync`.
- An error state with Retry (`ErrorContent`, `IzzyText.LoadFailed`).
- Rows stack into cards on a phone (`SmallScreen`, `StackBelow`); `RowCurrent`, `RowHover`, `ShadedHeader`, `FilterPanel`.
- A header sorts up, down, then not at all; sorted columns show plain arrows.
- A page of rows keeps its height as filters leave fewer.

## Other changes

- **Fields:** `Clearable` on TextField, TextArea, NumberField, Select, MultiSelect and Combobox puts a clear button in the field.
- `Color.SurfaceSunken` for areas that hold cards. In dark mode the page sits just under the surface, and overlays sit under the fills.
- `DialogOptions.Width`; a Dialog uses the page's surface rather than a raised one.
- `FormErrors.ExceptFields`; a value a named form can't read fails its post.
- `Pagination.ClearKeys`.
- Menu items show their shortcuts as a description (`aria-keyshortcuts`), not part of their name. Menus and panels use the default placement and flip on their own.
- Kbd has a soft fill and a border.
- An open Select's list and other scrolling areas have themed scrollbars; icons with a straight line draw sharp at 1x.
- Row menus stay open after a right-click on macOS and Linux.
