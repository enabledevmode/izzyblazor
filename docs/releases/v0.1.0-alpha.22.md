Charts (fourteen types, with zoom, drill-down, live data and synced charts), a Scheduler, a Chat, a rich text editor, tree selects, and an accessibility pass over every component with a new Accessibility section in the docs. Several components were split where one did two jobs, and parameters that broke the library's conventions were renamed, so read the Breaking list before upgrading: Razor accepts an old parameter name without a warning.

## Breaking

- **Split into their own components:** a password field is `PasswordField` (TextField's `Reveal`, `ShowPasswordLabel` and `Requirements` moved there, `TextFieldType.Password` is gone, and TextField refuses a `[DataType(DataType.Password)]` property); a month or a year is `MonthField` or `YearField` (`DateFieldType.Month` and `.Year` are gone; Calendar's `Type` is now a `CalendarType`); a floating action button is `FloatingButton` (`Floating` and `FloatPosition` are gone from every button, and the enum is `FloatPlacement`, set by `Placement`); link tabs are `TabNav` with `TabLink`s (Tabs holds only `Tab`); a menu with no button is `ContextMenu` (a `Menu` needs a `Label` or a `Trigger`); theming part of a page is `ThemeScope` (`IzzyTheme` renders only the head tags and no longer takes `ChildContent`, `Class` or `Style`).
- **Toggles are checkboxes:** `Pressed` and `PressedChanged` are gone from Button, IconButton, ConfirmButton, CopyButton, DialogTrigger, DialogClose, ToolbarButton and ToolbarIconButton. Use `ToggleButton` (or `ToolbarToggleButton` in a Toolbar), and `ToggleGroup` for several.
- **Table and DataGrid columns go in `<Columns>`**, not directly in the grid. DataGrid's `EditLayout` and `EditFormTemplate` parts are replaced by the `EditDialog` and `EditDialogForm` slots and the grid's `EditColumns`, `EditLayout`, `EditDialogSize` and `EditDialogTitle`.
- **Renamed:** `Popover.Mode` → `AutoClose` (true by default; `PopoverMode` is gone), `Icon.Role` → `Purpose` (`IconRole` → `IconPurpose`), `Heading.Outline` → `OnThisPage`, `Anchor.Inherit` → `InheritColor`, `Barcode.ModuleWidth` → `BarWidth`, `DescriptionList.Divided` → `Dividers`, `Diff.Context` → `ContextLines`, `PinField.Masked` → `Secret`, `Step.Hidden` → `Visible` (inverted), `ListView.AriaLabel` and `TreeView.AriaLabel` → `Label`, `TreeView.Expanded` → `ExpandedItems`, Table's and DataGrid's `FilterFromAddress` → `FilterFromQuery`, DataGrid's `OpenDetails`/`DetailsOpen` → `ExpandedDetails`/`ExpandAllDetails` and `OpenGroups`/`GroupsOpen` → `ExpandedGroups`/`ExpandAllGroups`, and `ShowText` → `ShowLabel` on DataGridAdd, DataGridColumnPicker, DataGridExport and DataGridFilterBuilder.
- **Sticky bars:** `Position` on Banner, AppHeader and AppFooter is replaced by `Sticky` (a bool), and `BarPosition` is gone.
- **ColorPicker:** `Presets` and `PresetText` are `Swatches` and `SwatchText`, and `PresetsLabel` is gone; the swatch opens a ColorPanel in a popover.
- **Slider:** `ShowValue` is gone; use `ValueTooltip` (over the thumb) and `ShowEnds` (Min and Max at the track's ends).
- **Image throws without `Alt`.** Pass `Alt=""` for a picture that only decorates.
- **PasswordField sets `autocomplete="current-password"`** by default, or `new-password` when it has `Requirements`; set `AutoComplete` to change it.

## New components

- **Charts:** `Chart` with bar, line, area, scatter, bubble and waterfall series (`ChartBarSeries` and the others), axes (`ChartArgumentAxis`, `ChartValueAxis`, a second value axis, log scales), `ChartTooltip`, reference lines and bands, annotations, trend lines, value labels and a Show data table; `PieChart` (and donut), `NestedDonutChart`, `FunnelChart`, `HeatmapChart`, `CalendarHeatmap`, `RadarChart`, `SankeyChart`, `TreemapChart`, `Gauge` and `Sparkline`. All are drawn on the server as SVG and read with JavaScript off. With IzzyBlazor's script: tooltips and keys, legend toggles, zoom (`View`, drag, wheel, pinch and keys, or a `ChartRangeSelector` strip), `Sync` to zoom and hover several charts together, drill-down by address, and live data (`Refresh`). `RangeSelector` on its own chooses a range for anything.
- **Scheduler:** day, week, month and agenda views with add, edit and delete by form, categories, drag to move, templates, a toolbar of its own parts, and loading only the days shown.
- **Chat** and **ChatEntry:** a conversation with a composer that posts on static pages, streamed replies, attachments and people's messages in bubbles.
- **RichTextEditor** (a field that posts HTML, with Markdown shortcuts, links, images, tables, code blocks, task lists and its own undo), **RichText** (sanitized HTML shown as prose, with `RichTextSanitizer`) and **Markdown**.
- **TreeSelect** and **MultiTreeSelect:** choose from a tree in a field, with children that load.
- **ColorPanel:** swatches, a picker, opacity and fields, inline.
- **RangeSlider** (two thumbs, bound to a `NumberRange`), **ProgressCircle**, **SpeedDial**, **Blockquote**.
- **LiveRegion**, `Announcer` and **AnnouncerRegion:** tell screen reader users what changed, on interactive and static pages.
- **DynamicColumn** (columns known only at run time) and **ColumnBand** (headers over groups of columns) for Table and DataGrid.

## Other changes

- **TextField `Mask`**, formatted as typed and on the server.
- **Slider** `Orientation` and `Height`, `ValueTooltip`, `ShowEnds`, `Marks` and `ShowStepMarks`.
- **MultiSelect `AllowCustom`** takes typed values, such as labels or email addresses.
- **Tabs** `OnClose` and `OnAdd` close and add tabs, with links on static pages.
- **Menu `ContextFor`** opens a menu by right-click, long press or Shift+F10 as well as its button.
- **Form `ConfirmLeave`** asks before leaving a form with unsaved changes.
- **DataGrid:** a column menu (sort, add to sort, freeze, fit, hide, group), a column picker with search, bands and handles, and Alt+Left and Alt+Right to move a grouping level.
- **Filter panels** show "(Select all)", and the filter button stays lit while its panel is open.
- **Button `data-active`** shows as the accent and a dot (DataGrid's Filter button uses it).
- `Breakpoint.Xxl` (1536px), `AvatarSize.Xxs`, `--ib-chart-title-size` and `--ib-chart-title-weight`, richer code syntax colors, and `IzzyText` strings for the new components.
- Dialogs keep their layout while they close.

## Accessibility

- Focus shows on every control in Windows high-contrast mode, and progress bars, slider tracks and carousel dots keep their system colors there.
- A control that takes focus scrolls clear of AppShell's sticky header and footer, and of a Table's sticky header.
- Small buttons (a field's clear button, a tab's close button, TreeSelect's clear) take clicks from a 24-pixel area; the Scheduler's month appointments are 24 pixels tall.
- A light brand color keeps a focus ring with 3:1 contrast.
- Every component's keys are in its Keyboard table, and shortcuts outside the standard patterns are announced with `aria-keyshortcuts`.
- Table and DataGrid header buttons are named "Filter" and "Options", so a cell's column isn't read twice.

- Components are in 14 groups, each area of the docs has its own sidebar, and the components page shows every component as a card, A to Z.
