## Templates

- **Every generated app has a `README.md`:** how to run it, how its pages render, where things are, and how to add to it with `izzy`. An app with accounts says how to sign in; Izzy CRM's lists the sample team it makes, their password, and how to start with your own data.
- **`izzy new`** ends by pointing at the README.
