Every app that `izzy new` or `dotnet new` makes now comes with a README, and Izzy CRM tells you how to log in. Nothing was removed or renamed since alpha.24.

## Templates

- **Every generated app has a `README.md`:** how to run it, how its pages render, where things are, and how to add to it with `izzy`. An app with accounts says how to sign in.
- **Izzy CRM** lists its sample accounts, their password and where they come from in its README, and in development its log-in page names them too. The README also covers roles, starting with your own data, email and deploying.
- **`izzy new`** ends by pointing at the README.

## CLI

- **Pictures for front ends:** `izzy list --json` and `izzy describe <item> --json` give each item's page on the docs site (`docs`) and its picture in the light and dark scheme (`picture`). `describe` also gives a starter's or kit's `screens`: each with a caption, alt text, whether it's a phone's width, and the full picture and a thumbnail in both schemes. `izzy mcp`'s list_items and describe_item carry the same.
- **Labels for forms:** every setting in `izzy describe <item> --json` has a `title` in words ("Render mode", "Primary link"), so a form doesn't show the setting's name as code writes it.

## Docs site

- Signed in to your IzzyBlazor account, the site's header shows the account, and its pages stop offering to create one.
