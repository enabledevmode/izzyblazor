A small release for settings pages: a switch or checkbox can sit at the end of its row, and a `Fieldset` can draw lines between its rows. A long value in a `Select` now stays on one line. No renames.

## Changed (check when you update)

- **A long value in a `Select` stays on one line.** In Chrome and Edge (the customizable select), a value too long for the field used to wrap onto a second line that spilled out of the field. It now stays on one line and is cut off at the field's edge, as in the browser's classic select; with an `ItemTemplate`, its text ends in "…". Options in the open list still wrap. Give a field with long values more room (`ColSpan`) if the whole value matters.
- **New parameter `ControlPosition` on `Checkbox` and `Switch`.** A component of your own that inherits from `Checkbox` or `Switch` and declares its own `ControlPosition` parameter now throws when it renders: Blazor doesn't allow two parameters with one name. Rename yours, or use the inherited one.

## New

- **`ControlPosition` on `Checkbox` and `Switch`.** `ControlPosition="ControlPosition.End"` puts the box or switch at the end of the row, centered on the label and hint, with the hint under the label, as in a list of settings. The label text still toggles it and names it for screen readers, and errors show under the hint without moving it. Right-to-left pages flip it. The default, `Start`, is unchanged.
- **`Dividers` on `Fieldset`**: a line between its rows, midway in the `Gap`. Each direct child is a row. It's ignored when `Columns` is more than 1. With `ControlPosition.End`, it makes a settings list: `<Fieldset Label="Email notifications" Dividers>` around a few `<Switch ControlPosition="ControlPosition.End" Hint="…" />`.
